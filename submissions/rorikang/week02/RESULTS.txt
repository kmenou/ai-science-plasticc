Why the redshift shift is worse than a plain extrapolation problem.
  Among objects with photo-z > 0.5, the correction (z_spec - z_photo) they need is
      525 training objects (z_spec < 0.4): median -1.949
     1005 test objects (z_spec >= 0.4): median -0.006
  Cutting the training set on the target variable means every high-photo-z
  training object is a photo-z overestimate. The model learns to subtract
  roughly 2 from a high photo-z, and that is precisely wrong for the test
  domain, where a high photo-z is simply correct. This is why the residual
  parameterisation rescues the depth shift but not this one.
